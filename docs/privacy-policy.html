PDFly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

PDFly ("the app") is a PDF utility app developed by Nexus Digital Labs. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

How the app works

All PDF and image processing — compressing, merging, splitting, and converting images to PDF — happens entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to a server, and PDFly does not require an account, sign-in, or internet connection to process your documents.

Information we collect

PDFly itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or the contents of your files.

Advertising

PDFly displays ads through Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and other information for the purpose of showing and measuring ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. This is standard for apps using Google's advertising services and is not information PDFly itself collects or has access to.

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another region with similar requirements, PDFly shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the app, before any ad is personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options within the app.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

Permissions

PDFly requests access to files/photos you explicitly select through the system file or photo picker, solely to process them within the app. It does not access any other files or media on your device.

Children's privacy

PDFly is a general-purpose utility app not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
